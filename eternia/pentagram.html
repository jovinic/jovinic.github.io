DOSSIER: The Pentagram Paradigm (An Arcane Architecture)

I. The Core Components (The Five Runes)

The Pentagram Paradigm visualizes reality as a system built upon five foundational pillars. The first four are physical components, representing the building blocks of matter. The fifth is the logic operator, dictating how the physical components interact with the laws of the universe.

  • Earth (Solidity): Rigidity, mass, durability, cohesion, physical weight.
  • Water (Fluidity): Liquid state, flow, adaptability, temperature reduction.
  • Fire (Energy): Heat, combustion, particle acceleration, volatility.
  • Air (Dispersion): Gaseous state, diffusion, weightlessness, separation.
  • Ether (The Operator): The system framework, abstract logic, mind, soul, and the binding of reality's rules.

The Ether Variations:
The Ether Rune is unique because it never operates in a neutral physical state. It must be channeled through one of two metaphysical alignments that dictate the fate of a rule or property:

  • Holy Ether (The Protector): Embraces Order. It protects, reinforces, and anchors the rules of reality. It locks definitions into place so they cannot be easily altered or denied.
  • Occult Ether (The Doomsayer): Embraces Chaos. It dooms, unravels, and severs the rules of reality. It corrupts existing definitions, breaking down logical cause-and-effect.

II. The Two Tiers of Execution

The power and nature of the spell depend entirely on how the physical components are combined with the logic of the Ether variations.

Tier 1: Surface (Only 1 Rune)

Using a single rune does not rewrite reality; it operates within the existing boundaries of physics or abstract thought.

  • Physical Surface (Elemental Tinkering): Using one of the four physical runes alone merely amplifies or redirects forces that already obey natural physics.
    Example: Drawing the Water Rune alone to pull ambient moisture from the air and condense it into a splash of water.
  • Ether Surface (Abstract Logic): Using the Ether Rune without a physical component applies Holy or Occult logic strictly to metaphysical, invisible, or mental concepts.
    Truth Magic (Holy Ether): Utilized to ascertain the truth of various facts—checking if certain beliefs or statements match up with the genuine state of reality. Extremely advanced practitioners can even speak truth into being (minor prophecy).
    Madness Magic (Occult Ether): Chaos mages excel at influencing the mind, which is highly flexible. Casting Occult Ether directly into an enemy's mind shares peculiarly unpredictable forms of insanity, deleting their logical cause-and-effect processing.

Tier 2: Synthesis

This is True Arcane magic. By synthesizing physical runes with the logic of the Ether Rune, a magi can temporarily break, preserve, or permanently rewrite the fundamental properties of an object.

Single Synthesis (1 Ether Variation + Physical Rune):
Used for absolute preservation or destructive/temporary overrides.

Stasis Magic (Holy Ether + Physical Rune): Order magic capable of preserving things in an unchanging form, locked away from any possible influence of time. It reinforces the reality of an object to such an extent that it proves nigh-impossible to alter. (Cannot be used on the living; stasis is death in an inherently dynamic biological system).
Contract/Law Magic (Holy Ether + Physical Rune): The ability to bind individuals who agree to contracts. It locks the agreement into the code of reality. (Note: The powers of Helheim utilize advanced contract magic in their nefarious deal-making, proving Order is not always synonymous with good).
Decomposition Magic (Occult Ether + Earth Rune): A form of destroying things that does not simply eradicate them, but reduces them to their components (e.g., turning a solid tree into raw wood, water, and light). It dooms the rule holding the object together.
Alteration Magic (Occult Ether + Water Rune): Chaos eats away at raw sense and logic. Alteration distorts reality, influencing non-living things to take another form (e.g., unnaturally forcing fluidity onto solid stone). Things created this way are incompatible with reality's rules and cannot exist permanently, but do massive damage on their way out.

Dual Synthesis (Both Ether Variations + Physical Rune):
Used for true creation and permanent reformation. This requires synthesizing both Holy and Occult Ether simultaneously.

Transmutation (Inanimate Objects): Targeting a heavy iron shield, the magi casts the Occult Ether Rune to doom and unmake its innate Earth property (weight). Simultaneously, they cast the Holy Ether Rune containing the Air Rune to write and protect its new density tag.
Transformation (Living Beings): The magi uses Occult Ether to doom a creature's biological rule of defense, while forcing a Holy Ether imbued with the Water Rune to rewrite the tissue as soft and malleable. This violently overrides biology, permanently leaving a metaphysical scar on the creature's soul and body.

III. The Three Laws

Eternia's strict rules govern how the paradigm can be applied:

  1. Conservation of Mass (The Imprint Rule): Runes cannot be conjured into a vacuum to create matter. An Ether override must always be anchored to an existing target to alter its definitions.
  2. Difficulty Scales with Power (The Resistance Rule): Overwriting the properties of mundane materials costs minimal mana. Trying to force an Ether override on an Arcanium blade or a living being requires fighting the object's immense innate logic, costing exponential amounts of mana and focus.
  3. Larger Changes Incur Larger Costs (Conceptual Distance): Shifting an object to an adjacent physical state is manageable. Dooming a property to impose its exact opposite requires a massive logic rewrite and takes a tremendous toll on the caster.

IV. Casting Methodology

To execute this architectural approach to magic, the casting process is split into distinct, methodical physical actions:

  • The Framework (Drawing the Ether): The magi first traces the circular Ether framework in the air.
    • Single Synthesis: One ring—unbroken, radiant geometric gold for Holy Ether, or erratic, consuming abyssal violet for Occult Ether.
    • Dual Synthesis: Two overlapping, interlocking rings—one gold, one violet—like a cosmic Venn diagram.
  • The Component (Inserting the Rune): The specific physical rune (Earth, Water, Fire, Air) is drawn inside the framework (or within the intersection of the dual rings) to complete the spell's formula.
  • The Execution (The Stare): The magic is not thrown; it is assigned. By locking an unblinking, heavy stare onto the target, the magi acts as the tether. They drag the glowing code from the air and slam it into the object's core, forcing reality to accept the newly protected or doomed definition.
